What Makes Idaho Renters Insurance Unique

Idaho's rapid population growth in the Boise metro area has increased rental demand faster than insurers have adjusted pricing, so shopping around annually can still turn up meaningful savings.

Risk Factors Specific to Idaho

Underwriters in Idaho weigh wildfire, smoke damage, and evacuation-related loss more heavily than in lower-risk states when pricing renters policies.

Renters Insurance Compared With Rent in Idaho

The median gross rent in Idaho was $1,384 a month in 2024 (U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey), 7% below the national median of $1,487. Against that, our estimated renters premium of $128 a year — about $11 a month — equals roughly 9% of one month’s rent for a full year of coverage, below the middle share among the 50 states in our estimates.

The Coverage Detail That Matters Most in Idaho

In Idaho, the wildfire question for renters is usually about displacement rather than the building itself. Additional living expenses coverage pays for temporary housing if a covered fire makes your unit unlivable, and many policies also pay for a limited period when a civil authority bars access. Smoke damage to belongings is generally covered as part of fire. Check the limit on additional living expenses — in high-rent areas, a short displacement can exhaust a low limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is renters insurance required in Idaho?

No. No U.S. state, including Idaho, legally requires tenants to carry renters insurance. That said, individual landlords and property management companies in Idaho can — and often do — require proof of a policy as a lease condition, especially for larger apartment complexes.

How much does renters insurance cost in Idaho?

Renters in Idaho pay an estimated $128 per year ($11/month) for a standard policy, based on 2026 industry average data. Actual premiums vary based on coverage limits, deductible, credit history (where allowed), claims history, and the specific building or neighborhood.

Can insurers use your credit score to set renters insurance rates in Idaho?

Yes. Idaho allows insurers to factor in your credit-based insurance score when calculating renters insurance premiums, alongside coverage limits, claims history, and location. Renters with stronger credit typically qualify for lower rates from most carriers.

What's the biggest renters insurance risk factor in Idaho?

In Idaho, wildfire is the top concern. Standard renters policies do cover fire damage, including wildfire, but tenants in high-risk fire zones should confirm their insurer is still writing new policies in that area and consider replacement cost coverage given rising rebuild costs.

How much is renters insurance compared with rent in Idaho?

With a median rent of $1,384 a month (2024) and an estimated premium of $128 a year, a full year of renters insurance in Idaho costs roughly 9% of one month's rent.
