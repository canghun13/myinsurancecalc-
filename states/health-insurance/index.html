2027 Benchmark Premiums by State (Before Subsidies)

The average benchmark (second-lowest-cost silver) premium for a 40-year-old rose about 13%% for 2027, to $703 a month nationally, after a 26%% jump the year before. The table shows each state’s 2027 average, the change from 2026, and an estimate for a family of four (two 40-year-old adults and two children under 15) using the federal default age curve. Households above 400%% of the federal poverty level pay these prices in full in 2027; below that, the premium tax credit lowers them. Each state guide breaks the price down by age.
