Health Insurance Overview in Oklahoma

Oklahoma expanded Medicaid through a 2020 voter ballot initiative (State Question 802) that passed with 50.5% of the vote — the narrowest margin of any expansion ballot initiative. Expansion took effect in 2021. Oklahoma uses the federal HealthCare.gov marketplace. Oklahoma had been one of the few remaining non-expansion states before 2021.

Three things decide what you actually pay. Income: below 400%% of the federal poverty level, the premium tax credit cuts the benchmark plan to a set share of income; above it, you pay these prices in full, because the temporary rules that capped premiums at 8.5%% of income ended after 2025. Metal level: bronze plans cost less, and in many areas the cheapest gold plan costs about the same as or less than benchmark silver (nationally $694 versus $703 in 2027), so compare before defaulting to silver. County: these are statewide averages, and your county’s benchmark can be well above or below them. Self-employed? Our self-employed health insurance calculator estimates the after-tax cost.

Medicaid in Oklahoma

SoonerCare (Oklahoma Medicaid) expanded in 2021 following voter approval. Adults with incomes up to 138% of the federal poverty level are eligible.

Children's Health Insurance (CHIP) in Oklahoma

SoonerCare covers children under 19 in families with incomes up to 215% of the federal poverty level.

What Makes Oklahoma Unique

Oklahoma's Medicaid expansion passed by the narrowest margin of any voter-approved expansion (50.5%) but has proceeded successfully. Oklahoma's expansion covered approximately 200,000 previously uninsured Oklahomans. The close vote reflects the state's political divisions on healthcare policy.

ACA Subsidies: What Oklahoma Residents Should Know

The enhanced subsidies first enacted in the American Rescue Plan expired at the end of 2025. For 2026, premium tax credits follow the original ACA rules: there is no credit above 400% (the “subsidy cliff”), and enrollees are expected to pay up to 9.96% of income toward the benchmark Silver plan. House in January 2026 but has not become law, so 2027 plans are being priced and subsidized under the same rules unless Congress acts.

New for 2027: premium tax credits are limited to U.S. citizens, green card holders, Cuban/Haitian entrants and COFA migrants. Refugees, asylees, TPS holders and work or student visa holders can still enroll, but at full price — see 2027 ACA subsidies for immigrants.

Oklahoma’s Marketplace for 2027: Insurers, Rates, and Dates

Oklahoma residents enroll through HealthCare.gov (Oklahoma runs a state-based marketplace on the federal platform). Full-price premiums rose about 25.9% on average for 2026, before subsidies. 7 insurers sold plans for 2026; Medica and Mending Health are leaving for 2027, so those enrollees need to choose a new plan.

About 262,000 people enrolled for 2026. About 91% received premium tax credits, and the average net premium after credits was about $161 a month.

Open enrollment for 2027: November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027 on HealthCare.gov; enroll by December 15 for coverage starting January 1.

What’s Different About Health Coverage in Oklahoma

Oklahoma plans to move to its own enrollment platform in fall 2027 for 2028 coverage, with a reinsurance program planned for 2028 pending federal approval. For 2027, Medica and Mending Health exit (see what to do when your insurer leaves). Oklahoma expanded Medicaid by ballot initiative in 2021.

Tips for Choosing Health Insurance in Oklahoma

Oklahoma residents who were uninsured before 2021 should check SoonerCare Medicaid eligibility. HealthCare.gov subsidies are available for incomes 100-400% of the federal poverty level. SoonerCare provides children's coverage. Oklahoma's rural areas may have limited marketplace options.

  • Enroll during open enrollment (November 1 – January 15 (federal marketplace)) — missing the deadline means waiting until the next open enrollment unless you have a qualifying life event.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which insurers are leaving the Oklahoma marketplace for 2027?

Medica and Mending Health are leaving for 2027. Enrollees with those insurers should choose a new plan during open enrollment; an automatic replacement may have a different network or drug list.

When is open enrollment in Oklahoma for 2027?

November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027 on HealthCare.gov; enroll by December 15 for coverage starting January 1.

How much do Oklahoma marketplace enrollees pay after subsidies?

In 2026 about 91% of Oklahoma enrollees received premium tax credits, and the average net premium was about $161 a month. There is no credit above 400% of the federal poverty level.

How much does health insurance cost in Oklahoma in 2027?

Before subsidies, the average benchmark silver plan in Oklahoma costs $686 a month for a 40-year-old in 2027 (KFF). Using the federal age curve, that is roughly $537 at age 21, $1,457 at age 60 and $2,193 for a family of four with two young children. Households above 400% of the poverty level pay the full price; below that, the premium tax credit lowers it.
