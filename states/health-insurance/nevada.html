Health Insurance Overview in Nevada

Nevada operates Nevada Health Link, a state-based marketplace. Nevada expanded Medicaid in 2014. Nevada launched a state public option, the Battle Born State Plans, for the 2026 plan year. Nevada's marketplace has competitive options in the Las Vegas and Reno metro areas.

What Makes Nevada Unique

Nevada's public option launched for the 2026 plan year as the Battle Born State Plans, part of a Market Stabilization Program that also added reinsurance. Nevada is one of only a few states with a public option on its marketplace.

ACA Subsidies: What Nevada Residents Should Know

The enhanced subsidies first enacted in the American Rescue Plan expired at the end of 2025. For 2026, premium tax credits follow the original ACA rules: there is no credit above 400% (the “subsidy cliff”), and enrollees are expected to pay up to 9.96% of income toward the benchmark Silver plan. Battle Born State Plans: Nevada’s Public Option Is Live

Nevada’s public option, the Battle Born State Plans, became available on Nevada Health Link starting with 2026 coverage, as part of the state’s Market Stabilization Program. The program pairs the public option with a reinsurance program approved through a federal Section 1332 waiver; reinsurance pays part of the cost of very expensive claims, which lets insurers file lower full-price premiums than they otherwise would. Nevada became the 18th state with reinsurance as of 2026.

Reinsurance helps people who pay full price most. If you receive a large premium tax credit, a lower benchmark can actually shrink your credit, so compare net prices rather than assuming the public option is cheapest for you.

2026 Prices and Nevada’s 2027 Insurers

  • Full-price premiums rose about 22.3% on average for 2026, ranging from about 3.1% (Hometown Health) to 26.9% (Imperial).
  • Nine insurers offer 2027 plans: CareSource Nevada, HMO Nevada, Community Care Health Plans, Health Plan of Nevada, SelectHealth, SilverSummit, Hometown Health Plan, Imperial Insurance, and Molina Healthcare.
  • Open enrollment for 2027 coverage runs November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027 on Nevada Health Link.

Networks vary sharply between Las Vegas and Reno and in rural counties. Check that your doctors and nearest hospital are in-network before choosing on price.

Tips for Choosing Health Insurance in Nevada

Nevada residents should enroll through Nevada Health Link rather than HealthCare.gov. Medicaid covers low-income adults. Las Vegas and Reno have competitive insurer options. Nevada's public option, the Battle Born State Plans, is now one of the choices worth comparing.

  • Enroll during open enrollment (November 1 – January 15 (Nevada Health Link)) — missing the deadline means waiting until the next open enrollment unless you have a qualifying life event.
