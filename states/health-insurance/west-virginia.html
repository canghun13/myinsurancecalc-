Health Insurance Overview in West Virginia

West Virginia expanded Medicaid in 2014 and saw one of the most dramatic reductions in uninsured rate of any state — from approximately 20% to under 7% by 2016. West Virginia uses the federal HealthCare.gov marketplace. The state has limited marketplace competition given its small population and rural character. West Virginia has significant healthcare access challenges in rural areas.

Medicaid in West Virginia

West Virginia Medicaid expanded in 2014, covering adults with incomes up to 138% of the federal poverty level. The expansion dramatically reduced the uninsured rate.

Children's Health Insurance (CHIP) in West Virginia

WV CHIP covers children under 19 in families with incomes up to 300% of the federal poverty level.

What Makes West Virginia Unique

West Virginia's Medicaid expansion reduced the state's uninsured rate by more than 13 percentage points — one of the largest reductions in the nation. The expansion was particularly impactful given West Virginia's high rates of substance use disorders and mental health conditions, providing access to behavioral health services for previously uninsured residents.

ACA Subsidies: What West Virginia Residents Should Know

The enhanced subsidies first enacted in the American Rescue Plan expired at the end of 2025. For 2026, premium tax credits follow the original ACA rules: there is no credit above 400% (the “subsidy cliff”), and enrollees are expected to pay up to 9.96% of income toward the benchmark Silver plan. West Virginia’s Marketplace for 2027: Insurers, Rates, and Dates

West Virginia residents enroll through HealthCare.gov. Full-price premiums rose about 12.2% on average for 2026, before subsidies. 2 insurers sold plans for 2026; for 2027, CareSource is leaving and Peak Health is entering. Proposed 2027 changes average about 18.3% (Highmark) before subsidies.

About 56,000 people enrolled for 2026. About 89% received premium tax credits, and the average net premium after credits was about $208 a month.

What’s Different About Health Coverage in West Virginia

West Virginia has the highest average full-price marketplace premiums in the country — and therefore the largest credits, averaging about $1,244 a month in 2026. That makes the 400% FPL cliff especially severe here. CareSource exits for 2027 and Peak Health enters alongside Highmark. Medicaid expansion adults face the federal work requirement starting in 2027.

Tips for Choosing Health Insurance in West Virginia

West Virginia Medicaid covers low-income adults comprehensively, including behavioral health services — important given the state's high rates of substance use disorders. Marketplace options may be limited — comparing all available plans is critical. WV CHIP provides children's coverage. Telehealth is particularly valuable for rural West Virginians with limited provider access.

  • Enroll during open enrollment (November 1 – January 15 (federal marketplace)) — missing the deadline means waiting until the next open enrollment unless you have a qualifying life event.
