Walmart Marketplace Insurance Requirements for Sellers

6 min read · Updated November 2026

Walmart Marketplace has a simpler insurance rule than Amazon, but a higher bar once it applies. Sellers must submit a certificate of insurance (COI) when they exceed $100,000 in gross merchandise value (GMV) in any 12-month period, or whenever Walmart notifies them that one is required. The policy must carry general and product liability limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate — double Amazon’s aggregate.

The Exact Certificate Wording

Walmart accepts either of two ways to show itself on the COI. Use one of them word for word:

OptionWhere it goes on the certificateWording
1. Certificate holderCertificate holder boxWalmart Inc., 702 SW 8th Street, Bentonville, AR 72716-3570, Attn: Insurance Compliance
2. Additional insuredDescription of operationsWalmart Inc., its subsidiaries and its affiliates are listed as additional insured

The two options are not equal in what they do. A certificate holder simply receives proof of your policy; an additional insured gets coverage under it for claims arising from your products. If you choose option 2, the policy needs an actual additional insured endorsement — typing the words on the certificate doesn’t create coverage. For retail distribution, the standard tool is a vendors endorsement (ISO form CG 20 15), which extends your products coverage to a retailer that sells them. Many insurers offer a blanket additional insured endorsement that covers any party you are required by written contract to add, which handles Walmart, Amazon and wholesale buyers with one form. Because Walmart’s aggregate is higher, a policy built only to Amazon’s minimum ($1M/$1M) won’t pass. The usual fix is a general liability policy with a $2,000,000 general aggregate and a $2,000,000 products-completed operations aggregate, which is a common standard form limit and often costs little more than $1M/$1M. An umbrella policy on top can also satisfy higher aggregate demands from big-box retailers.

Why COIs Get Rejected

  • Name mismatch. “Acme Goods” on the policy and “Acme Goods LLC” in Seller Center is a mismatch. Fix the policy, not the account.
  • Aggregate too low. A $1,000,000 aggregate fails the $2,000,000 requirement.
  • No products coverage. Some small-business general liability policies exclude or sublimit products-completed operations. It must be included at the full limits.
  • Wrong Walmart entity or address. Use Walmart Inc. and the Bentonville address exactly.
  • Expired or soon-expiring policy. Upload the renewal certificate before the old one lapses.

What It Costs

Walmart doesn’t sell insurance, so you buy the policy from any insurer or broker. Because the trigger is $100,000 in sales, Walmart sellers who need a COI are past the minimum-premium stage where per-unit costs hurt most. Product category drives price far more than the platform: home goods and apparel are usually inexpensive to insure, while supplements, baby products, lithium batteries and anything ingested or applied to skin cost more. A single policy listing all of your sales channels — Walmart, Amazon, your own store and wholesale — is normally cheaper than separate policies. Frequently Asked Questions

Does Walmart Marketplace require insurance?

Yes, once a seller exceeds $100,000 in gross merchandise value in any 12-month period, or whenever Walmart notifies the seller that a certificate of insurance is required. Walmart encourages sellers below the threshold to carry coverage too.

What insurance limits does Walmart require for sellers?

General liability and product liability of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, with the legal business name on the policy matching the name in Seller Center.

How should Walmart be listed on my certificate of insurance?

Either as certificate holder at Walmart Inc., 702 SW 8th Street, Bentonville, AR 72716-3570, Attn: Insurance Compliance, or in the description of operations with Walmart Inc., its subsidiaries and its affiliates listed as additional insured.

What happens if I don't submit a COI to Walmart?

Walmart says processing may be delayed, the account may be inactivated for lack of coverage, and orders and payments may be withheld until the certificate is approved.

Will my Amazon insurance policy work for Walmart?

Only if it meets Walmart's limits. Amazon requires $1 million per occurrence and $1 million aggregate, while Walmart requires a $2 million aggregate. A general liability policy with $1 million per occurrence and $2 million aggregates, listing both platforms, usually satisfies both.
